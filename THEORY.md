# Theory and Research Scope

**Project:** `Fractalplicitive::Math` | **Release:** 0.01  
**Original theoretical framework:** Timothy Dean Firman  
**Status:** Exploratory mathematical and simulation software; physical hypotheses unvalidated.  
**Related:** [Equations](EQUATIONS.md) · [Validation](VALIDATION.md) · [References](REFERENCES.md)

## 1. Purpose

`Fractalplicitive::Math` translates selected formulas in Timothy Dean Firman's unpublished *Fractalplicitive Theories* and *Fractalplicitive Inward-Signal Reception and Recursive Echo Reconstruction* (FISRARER) manuscripts into executable Perl. The module makes the formulas inspectable, repeatable, and comparable with conventional methods; it does **not** establish a new physical law.

The research program proposes investigating recursive or self-similar relationships across time coordinates, spatial scales, energy-like variables, waveforms, information, and interferometric geometry. The implementation is a **numerical research prototype**, not an instrumentation or control system.

## 2. What is original, and what is already known?

| Concept | Status in this package | Scientific interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric series, complex numbers, `mc²`, four sinusoids, pairwise baselines | Established mathematics / established physics where applicable | The code evaluates familiar mathematical expressions. |
| Fractalplicitive time recurrence | Equation from Firman's manuscript | Simulated indexing/coordinate progression; not proof that clock time physically behaves this way. |
| Recursive observation schedule | Practical extension in FISRARER | Candidate strategy for sampling synthetic or recorded signals. |
| Inverse momentum scaling | Proposal from Firman's manuscript | Numerical scaling of an input value; **not** a measured modification of photon momentum. |
| Complex energy/displacement recurrences | Proposal from Firman's manuscript | Dimensionless mathematical dynamics, **not** evidence of tachyons or physical amplification. |
| Tetraformed harmonic field | Classical four-oscillator analogue | No claim that sound waves create matter, or that binaural perception occurs in vacuum. |
| Echo model | Ordinary discrete delayed-signal sum | Can synthesize known echoes; cannot infer an intelligent sender from a pattern. |
| Interferometric aperture geometry | Established combinatorial and geometric calculations | **No** fringe simulation, imaging inversion, or photon measurement is implemented in v0.01. |
| Fractalplicitive rotating-array schedule | Proposed numerical observation geometry | Returns scheduled times and angles; **no** motor or aircraft interface. |
| `mc³` | A computed quantity motivated by the speculative third-order layer | Has units of energy × speed, **not** energy; no novel energy law is claimed. |

## 3. Framework and research branches

### 3.1 Recursive time

Firman's original time recurrence applies a scale-dependent multiplier to the previous time value. In v0.01 it is represented by `fractal_time_step` and `fractal_times`. If initialized at zero, this recurrence remains zero, which is an important feature of the actual equation, not a bug concealed by implementation.

FISRARER separately describes a schedule of **increments** generated by a geometric progression. `recursive_schedule` is an engineering-style observation timetable and **must not** be confused with the original recurrence. See [EQUATIONS.md](EQUATIONS.md#1-recursive-time-and-observation-schedules).

### 3.2 Inverse scaling

Firman's *Fractalplicitive Inverse Theory* introduces sums of inverse geometric factors applied to photon momentum. This release can calculate the numerical factor, a scaled numerical momentum, and normalized weights for exploratory analysis. A scaling factor in software is not independently a physical energy source or a new photon law.

### 3.3 Complex-valued experimental models

The original manuscript includes recursive expressions for an energy-like variable and spatial-displacement-like variable with complex factors. Because complex powers of dimensionful quantities are not physically meaningful without a reference scale, v0.01 evaluates these on **normalized, dimensionless inputs**. Any mapping to calibrated measurements would require an explicit physical model, dimensional constants, independent data, and validation.

### 3.4 Harmonic and signal models

The proposed tetraformed/binaural terminology is represented by four ordinary cosine waves added together (`tetra_wave`). The FISRARER echo idea is represented by a discrete tapped-delay model (`echo_response`). These provide an accessible platform for studying phase, interference, timing, and signal reconstruction with known test inputs.

The proposed fifth-harmonic echo, inverse-fourth relationship, mirrored cones, sub-quark formation, tachyon detection, and physical cosmological mechanism are **not implemented as verified physical models** in this release.

### 3.5 Telescope and instrument geometry

`aperture_pair_count` and `aperture_baselines` describe pair counts and two-dimensional baseline vectors for conceptual aperture arrays. `rotation_states` calculates a sequence of observation times and commanded angles using two recursive factors.

These functions **do not** implement optical propagation, calibration, image reconstruction, aperture-mask manufacturing, astronomical lookback-time inference, or real hardware control. Their role is preparing numerical inputs for future controlled experiments.

### 3.6 Aircraft guidance: research only

The proposed FISRARER-AV application concerns offline analysis of navigation data and simulated anomalies. **This library must not be connected to flight-control or certified guidance systems.** It contains no real-time guarantees, validated fault handling, sensor drivers, uncertainty model, or safety certification.

## 4. Research workflow

1. **Thesis:** State a specific mathematical or physical claim and define all variables, reference units, and assumptions.
2. **Prosthesis (candidate mechanism):** Express the proposed relationship as executable code or a controlled simulation.
3. **Antithesis:** Compare with conventional mathematical models and identify alternate explanations.
4. **Validation:** Construct unit tests, numerical cross-checks, baseline comparisons, negative controls, and falsification criteria.
5. **Synthesis:** Retain, revise, or reject the candidate model based on results; document all outcomes.

This is an **instructional organization**, not a substitute for peer review or a claim of a new scientific method.

## 5. Questions appropriate for external review

- Does inverse recursive weighting improve any *defined* reconstruction task when compared with ordinary weighting at matched compute and noise levels?
- Do recursively scheduled measurements improve detection or reduce error relative to uniform or optimized sampling?
- Can a proposed physical interpretation be made dimensionally correct and yield predictions different from established theory?
- What measurable outcome would falsify each Fractalplicitive hypothesis?
