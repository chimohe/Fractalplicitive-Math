Introduction

Fractalplicitive Theories is an original interdisciplinary research framework developed by Timothy Dean Firman to investigate recursive relationships among time, space, energy, momentum, harmonic interactions, and information processing.

Unlike conventional fractal mathematics, which primarily investigates self-similarity, recursive structures, and scaling properties, Fractalplicitive Mathematics proposes extending recursive mathematical models to time-dependent and physical systems.

The purpose of this GitHub repository is to make selected Fractalplicitive mathematical equations publicly accessible through executable Perl code, allowing independent researchers to examine, reproduce, test, criticize, and further develop the proposed models.

Research Areas

The broader Fractalplicitive research program includes:

1. Fractalplicitive Time Theory: Recursive temporal progression and mathematical scaling.
2. Fractalplicitive Inverse Theory: Inverse recursive relationships involving momentum, energy, and numerical transformations.
3. Fractalplicitive Big Bang Theory: A speculative dual-layer cosmological model incorporating needle-to-threaded-cone geometry and hypothetical tachyonic interactions.
4. Fractalplicitive Quantum Time Telescope: Proposed multiple-aperture interferometry, recursive optical-array movement, and astronomical reconstruction.
5. FISRARER: Fractalplicitive Inward-Signal Reception and Recursive Echo Reconstruction, with potential applications in signal analysis, telecommunications, and navigation research.
6. Harmonic and Quantum Models: Exploratory relationships involving tetraformed waveforms, oscillatory convergence, and hypothetical particle interactions.

Software Implementation

Fractalplicitive::Math provides an initial computational implementation of selected equations and numerical models.

The Perl module includes recursive time calculations, inverse scaling, complex-valued mathematical recurrences, harmonic waveform simulations, echo models, interferometric aperture geometry, and rotating-array schedules.

The repository contains source code, automated tests, examples, mathematical documentation, and references to the originating research.

The software is intended for numerical experimentation and computational analysis, not operational flight control or other safety-critical applications.

Invitation for Scientific Review

I invite mathematicians, physicists, computer scientists, engineers, astronomers, and independent researchers to examine this work.

Scientific Status

Fractalplicitive Theories remains an exploratory research framework. Its proposed physical mechanisms have not been independently established through experimental observation.

The implementation of mathematical equations in software does not constitute validation of new physical laws.

This repository is intended to encourage transparent scientific examination, including evidence that may support, modify, or contradict the proposed hypotheses.

Dissemination Objective

The purpose of making Fractalplicitive::Math publicly available is to promote interdisciplinary scientific communication and provide a common computational foundation for independent investigation.

I welcome academic collaboration, technical review, and the dissemination of this research to qualified individuals and institutions capable of evaluating its mathematical and experimental implications.

Scientific progress depends upon the freedom to question established models, the responsibility to test new hypotheses, and the willingness to revise conclusions when confronted with evidence.
